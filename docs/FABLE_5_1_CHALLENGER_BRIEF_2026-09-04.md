## Operating model to test

Current candidate model:

**CREATE → CAPTURE → CONVERT → RECOVER → COMPOUND**

### CREATE
Paid acquisition · media buying · strategy · offers · copy · generative-AI creative · creative testing/CRO.

### CAPTURE
Websites · landing pages · funnel design · conversion-rate optimization · calls · forms · chat · CRM intake · speed-to-lead.

### CONVERT
AI voice · outbound follow-up · conversational automation · qualification · nurture · booking/handoff. Where supporting systems genuinely allow it, conversational surfaces may include SMS, email, WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram. Never imply a unified live deployment that cannot be verified.

### RECOVER
Missed calls · dormant leads · prior inquiries · aged opportunities · no-shows · reactivation campaigns.

### COMPOUND
CAC · allowable CAC · cost per qualified lead · booking/show/close rates · LTV · LTV:CAC · gross margin/profit · attribution · testing · feedback loops · AI agents.

Do not force this into five equal cards. Consider a control system, dual-input system, or flywheel if it communicates the truth faster.
