What I won't claim.

Every marketer will show you their best numbers. Almost none will show you the ones they threw out. Here's my discard pile — and the rule that put each one there. Hold the section above to this standard, then hold anyone else's to it.

A famous "before → after" laser CPL pair

A med spa laser turnaround I ran is real — but the exact before/after figures lived in an ad account I no longer have access to. Until it's re-sourced, I say "cut CPL substantially" and cite the ~$16 laser CPL verified on an account I still hold.

Client revenue & ROAS

My job ended at the qualified, booked appointment plus the follow-up system. Closing was the client's. I had no reliable post-booking revenue visibility — so I don't claim their revenue. Ever.

An exact Montreal CPL

The campaign's lead volume and bookings are tracker-corroborated (220+ leads, 89 booked in ~10 weeks), but the exact CPL decimal lived in a lost account — so the decimal stays retired, in every form, including a rounded one.

Blended "average CPL"

$225K+ managed and 6,250+ leads are both true — but dividing one by the other creates a fake CPL that blends brand spend, B2C, and qualified B2B. I quote CPL per campaign or not at all.

A shiny old e-commerce revenue figure

I built a six-figure Amazon/eBay business through college — founder-attested and I'll gladly talk through it. The precise lifetime figure predates retrievable records, so it stays out of the hard-claims column.

Calling a scheduled appointment a held call

My own B2B campaigns drove 215+ website-scheduled appointments. The platform can't verify who actually showed — so I say "scheduled," never something stronger. Precision is the point.

How this page stays honest

The verification system behind it.

This isn't a claims page with adjectives — it's the public face of a private, version-controlled evidence vault.

Source-system pulls

Headline metrics are pulled from the Meta Ads API at campaign level (spend, results, cost-per-result) — not from memory or old slides — and re-pulled before being called verified.

Dual-sourcing for bookings

Appointment claims are checked against two systems where possible (CRM + client tracking sheets). "Booked" means scheduled — I don't imply attendance.

A machine-readable claims contract

Every approved claim (and every banned phrasing) lives in a JSON contract; a linter scans my résumé, this site, and outreach copy so a retired number can't quietly come back.

Adversarial review

Claims get challenged before they ship — three were downgraded or retired after failing re-verification. The wall you see is what survived.
