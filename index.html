From strategy to implementation

I think through the whole path.
Then I build it.

A campaign is not a collection of disconnected tasks. The offer, creative, media, follow-up and measurement need to work together.

  1. 01 / Offer

    Find the reason to act.

    Understand the buyer, the business and what makes the next step worth taking.

  2. 02 / Creative + media

    Turn the idea into a test.

    Develop the angles, build the creative, structure the campaign and read the signal.

  3. 03 / Marketing operations

    Connect what happens next.

    Build the lead response, routing, CRM and follow-up around the customer journey.

  4. 04 / Measurement

    Work on the constraint.

    Look beyond cost per lead to booking, attendance, close-rate assumptions and unit economics.
