And now the same job, generated

The newest addition to the stack: UGC-style ads generated and directed rather than filmed. A hook on its own is a component — it stops the scroll and then asks for nothing. A direct-response ad is that hook plus the end card that carries the promise and the CTA. So here is the component, and here is the finished unit it builds into.

The end card is deliberately claim-free. Brand, promise, CTA — and not one number, because a figure printed into an ad has to trace to a sourced figure before it runs, and that sourcing is a separate job from the craft. The hook is the cut I signed off on; the card is my own build, standing in until the designer's final art lands.

Craft, not a performance claim. These are shown as creative work — you won't find a CPL or a ROAS bolted onto any of them here, because a creative isn't a result. The numbers I've earned live in The Proof Room, where every one names its source and its verification status.

And nothing identifying ships. Creatives carrying staff faces, name badges, clinic signage or client logos are held back — no client here signed a release for my portfolio, so they don't appear in it.
