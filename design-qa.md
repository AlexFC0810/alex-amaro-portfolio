## Content and evidence checks

- The public identity is **AI-Native Growth Operator**; the hiring translation is **Growth Marketing & AI Automation Lead**.
- The system is modeled as a graph: new demand and latent demand converge in the conversation layer; outcomes and economics feed learning upstream.
- Reputation is a post-service compounding loop, not an equal-width funnel stage.
- Omnichannel language is scoped to channels that are supported and configured.
- The live number is linked as `tel:+18888147785`, presented as interactive system proof, and explicitly separated from customer outcomes.
- Performance, system, and judgment proof are visually and semantically distinct.
- SEE IT / TRY IT / VERIFY IT is implemented as a repeatable proof rhythm.
- The CareLine animation work is excluded while it remains unfinished.
- Existing verified claim numbers resolve to the repository's claims contract; the route does not introduce a revenue, ROAS, held/sold, reputation-lift, or blended-CPL claim.

## Known tradeoff

The challenger intentionally links to the full evidence ledger rather than reproducing v4's twelve-row source accordion. This improves first-visit clarity and career/client optionality, but v4 remains the stronger destination for a reader who wants to audit every number without leaving the page. If promoted, retain `/v4/` as the deep-proof archive or fold its ledger into a secondary “verify every number” route.
